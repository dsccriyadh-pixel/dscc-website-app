Swimming Pool System

Why DSCC for Swimming Pool System in Riyadh

DSCC delivers Swimming Pool System across Riyadh and the wider Central Region with a local accountable team and a documented track record. We serve residential, commercial, hospitality, and infrastructure projects with full Saudi Building Code (SBC) and Civil Defense compliance — and provide a fixed BOQ-based quote after a site visit.

Our edge in this city

Local team in Riyadh

Our field teams operate from Riyadh directly — same-day site visits and one-business-day quotation, with no travel delays or remote project management.

Full Saudi Building Code (SBC) compliance

All designs and installations comply with SBC, NFPA, Saudi Civil Defense, and SASO standards. We coordinate inspections and provide As-Built documentation at handover.

Single accountable contractor

From design to handover, one team owns scope, schedule, and quality — instead of coordinating a dozen subcontractors.

Key features

  • Installation: Transform your space into a haven of aquatic delight with our expert swimming pool installation services. Whether you're dreaming of a serene backyard retreat or a commercial pool complex, we offer tailored solutions that encompass design, construction, and installation to bring your vision to life.
  • Maintenance: Proper maintenance is key to preserving the beauty and functionality of your swimming pool. Our comprehensive maintenance services cover everything from water quality testing and chemical balancing to filter cleaning and equipment inspections. By entrusting us with your pool's upkeep, you can enjoy crystal-clear waters year-round.
  • Repair: Even the most well-maintained swimming pools may encounter issues over time. Our technicians are well-equipped to diagnose and repair a wide range of pool-related problems, whether it's a malfunctioning pump, a leak, or issues with water circulation. Our prompt and efficient repair services ensure minimal downtime and maximum enjoyment.
  • Upgrade and Renovation: Enhance the aesthetics and functionality of your swimming pool with our upgrade and renovation services. From energy-efficient equipment replacements to modernizing pool finishes and adding exciting features like waterfalls or lighting, we can breathe new life into your pool.
